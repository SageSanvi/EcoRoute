🌿 Quick Guide

Here are three ways to give your items a better future:

🎨 Reuse

Use an item again or turn it into something new. Example: Make a planter from an old bottle.

♻️ Recycle

Send materials to be processed into new products. Example: Recycle an empty aluminum can.

🤝 Donate

Give usable items to someone who can use them. Example: Donate clothes you no longer wear.
